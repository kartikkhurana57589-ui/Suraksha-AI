🏛️ cybercrime.gov.in

National Cybercrime Reporting Portal

File an official complaint online ↗

While you report, also:

  • 1️⃣ Call your bank's official helpline to block cards / freeze UPI.
  • 2️⃣ Save screenshots, numbers, UPI IDs and transaction IDs as evidence.
  • 3️⃣ Do not send more money to "recover" funds — recovery-fee demands are a common second scam.

📱 I received a suspicious call / SMS / WhatsApp

No money lost yet? Report suspected fraud communication through the Government of India's Chakshu facility on Sanchar Saathi.

🛰️ Report via Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi) ↗

Also: block the sender, warn family members, and never call back unknown international numbers.

🔒 Important safety note

SURAKSHA and genuine officials will never ask for your OTP, PIN, password or banking credentials. Official links above go to Government of India portals. SURAKSHA is a guidance layer only and cannot guarantee recovery of lost money.

🤝 Trust & Transparency

SURAKSHA AI is a citizen safety/guidance layer — not a government system and not a replacement for law enforcement or official cybercrime reporting.

✅ SURAKSHA DOES

  • 🔍 Detect risk indicators in messages, links, numbers and payment requests
  • 💡 Explain WHY something looks risky, in simple words
  • 🛡️ Give immediate protection guidance
  • 🇮🇳 Provide vernacular education in 8 Indian languages
  • 🏛️ Navigate you to existing official reporting channels (1930, cybercrime.gov.in, Chakshu)
  • 📊 Demonstrate anonymised, synthetic trend dashboards

🚫 SURAKSHA DOES NOT

  • ❌ Guarantee that a message is or is not a scam
  • ❌ Replace police or law-enforcement processes
  • ❌ Perform or process financial transactions
  • ❌ Ask for passwords, OTPs, PINs or banking credentials
  • ❌ Guarantee recovery of lost money
  • ❌ Claim government partnership or certification

🧠 About the AI

Analysis runs through an abstracted service layer (analyzeScam(input)) returning structured, explainable results. This prototype uses a transparent rule-based signal engine; a production AI model can replace it behind the same interface, server-side, without exposing keys in the frontend. Results are probabilistic indicators — never certainty.
